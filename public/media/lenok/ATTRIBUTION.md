# Media attribution

The LenOK workshop, portrait, outdoor editorial and `process/process-04` photographs were supplied directly by the LenOK owner for this website update. They are stored locally and served as optimized JPEG/WebP variants. No price-list screenshot is used as gallery media and no face or body retouching was performed.

`process/process-01`, `process/process-02`, `process/process-03` and `process/process-05` are original, AI-generated editorial illustrations created for the missing process scenes. They contain no competitor branding and are clearly used as explanatory process imagery rather than documentary examples of completed LenOK work.

Code-native SVG/CSS garment and tailoring artwork remains original to this project. No third-party stock photography is used.
